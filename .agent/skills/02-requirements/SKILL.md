# Requirements and traceability

## Trigger
Use during the corresponding hackathon stage or when the user explicitly asks for it.

## Procedure
Before finalizing requirements, read QUESTIONS.md for clarifications. Distinguish host-specified requirements from organizer replies and your own interpretations; do not automatically make every reply a requirement.

Incorporate organizer answers supplied by the human; distinguish organizer-confirmed, host-documented, and provisional assumptions. Block P0 unknowns before design. Extract explicit requirements and constraints. Clearly label inferred quality expectations; define measurable acceptance tests and map each requirement to evidence.

## Deliverable
Update `.hackathon-harness/workspace/REQUIREMENTS.md`. Use the corresponding template if needed; record facts separately from assumptions.

## Stop condition
Stop when the stage output is decision-ready, or time budget expires. Escalate critical ambiguity; do not invent missing facts.

## 기록 원칙
담당자 질문·답변은 QUESTIONS.md에 간단히 기록합니다. 설계 선택은 DECISIONS.md에 실제 기술적 이유로 기록하며, 담당자 답변이 실제 영향을 준 경우에만 연결합니다.
